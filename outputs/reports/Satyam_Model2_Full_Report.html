1 · Summary & problemwhy this rebuild exists

Executive summary

Satyam Model 2 classifies culture-proven bacterial vs fungal keratitis from a single slit-lamp image. It is a ground-up rebuild of LVPEI's incumbent CornealAI Model 2, whose headline numbers are not trustworthy. On a patient-disjoint locked test it reaches AUC 0.815, and on 164 genuinely independent external cases 0.839 — the first uncontaminated measurement of this task. It reads pathology (not the camera), localises to the lesion, abstains when unsure, and shows the clinician what it saw.

The one-line result
On an 81%-fungal blind set it matched the incumbent on accuracy but made the sight-threatening error — a fungal ulcer called bacterial — zero times versus the incumbent's eight.

The incumbent problem

CornealAI Model 2's quoted AUC 0.949 is computed over all 686 images, 548 of which it trained on. Its best honest figure (0.862 / 77.5% on a 138-image split) is also contaminated: the bag builder inserts a lesion tile when the label is bacterial and a hypopyon tile when it is fungal — in training and validation — while inference does not, so the distributions differ. No independent test set, no patient grouping. Neither number is comparable to a clean held-out figure. That gap is what this project set out to close.

Design rules (held throughout)

  • Patient-grouped, repeated nested CV. One split has sd ≈0.015 (±0.03 noise) — enough to move a verdict a category. Every AUC is mean±sd over ≥8–10 fold assignments.
  • Nothing about the label touches preprocessing or tiling — the exact defect that invalidated the incumbent.
  • Aspect ratio preserved (letterbox, never tuple-resize — that distorts the limbus by a device-dependent factor).
  • Metrics at deployment prevalence, not the curated 1:1, and always with abstention.
  • Ties are called ties. Several promising ideas tied the baseline and were not adopted.

Evaluation protocol

Repeated nested cross-validation: an inner loop selects hyper-parameters on training folds only, an outer loop reports, over ≥8 fold assignments, quoted mean±sd. The locked 131-image test set was untouched from the first day until the single final evaluation. This discipline is why the numbers here can be trusted where the incumbent's cannot.

3 · Finding the architecturewhat worked, what tied, what failed

Where does the signal live?

A frozen DINOv2 probe (linear head only) established that whole-eye representations plateau at ~0.745 regardless of resolution or framing — global pooling averages the fine texture away. The lever turned out to be tile field of view. The scale curve is unimodal and peaks at 3.67 mm — which coincides with the 3.44 mm span of the feathery-margin sign in expert annotations, derived independently.

Regional ablation
The inferior third of the cornea alone — where the hypopyon and infiltrate sit — scores 0.786, ≈ the whole cornea's 0.791. The signal is concentrated in the disease region, exactly as clinical reasoning predicts.

Flagged clinically (subjective annotation; appears during healing → tracks treatment stage, not organism). Confirmed: 0.51 alone, and −0.002 when fused. It is kept out of both the classifier and the display overlay.

Interpretability, not accuracy

Biomarker fusion gave no accuracy gain (0.800→0.799) — the deep model already encodes what the geometric signs measure. So segmentation serves interpretability: the app outlines the detected infiltrate and hypopyon (outlines, so tissue stays visible) and shows clinical-sign chips, explicitly marked as review-only. The clinician can check the model against what they see.

6 · Limitations & next phasebefore retraining with more data

Honest limitations

  • Bacterial is the binding constraint. ~204 bacterial patients in the whole source; bacterial recall is weak in every cohort (73% / 62% / 51%). This is a data problem, not an architecture one — no modelling change fixed it.
  • Scope: binary only. The model cannot say normal / viral / scar. In a real review cohort 35% of images were out of scope and 27/35 got confident calls. Must be gated behind an infection detector before deployment.
  • Culture-positive only — culture-negative keratitis (common, earlier, milder) is unseen.
  • Prevalence. Cohorts are 51–64% fungal; clinic is fungal-heavy. At real prevalence a fungal call is reliable but a bacterial call is not — a bacterial output is a prompt to confirm.
  • Small test sets (131 internal, 164 external) → wide CIs; certifying 90% recall ±3% needs ~400 fungal test cases.
  • A minor display gap: the extreme-inferior hypopyon meniscus is under-tiled by the ≥50%-cornea rule (fixable, low risk).

The ceiling is data, not architecture

Every architectural lever — tile scale, resolution, pooling, lesion-guided tiling, biomarker fusion — has been explored; all but tile-scale and ensembling tied. The consistent conclusion: ~0.81–0.84 is what 682 culture-proven, image-only cases support.

Next phase — expansion

  • More culture-proven bacterial patients — the single biggest lever (≈10× a fungal one).
  • Additional organism classes — moving beyond binary toward the true clinical picture (e.g. viral, Acanthamoeba, mixed), which also addresses the "Others" scope gap.
  • Extend accrual past 2024, and add Vision-Centre captures (the deployment setting).
  • Intake covariates (trauma, contact-lens, duration, prior steroid) — information genuinely absent from the pixels, the most plausible route past the image-only ceiling.
  • Representation test still open: frozen ViT-B, or fine-tuning last blocks.
Status at this report
A trustworthy, calibrated, interpretable, fungal-safe model with the first honest numbers for the task. The foundation and evaluation harness are in place; the next gains come from data, and that is the work that follows this report.
